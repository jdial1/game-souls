## Pitfall: Tedious Backtracking

**Area:** Space & Pacing
**Found in:** components/interlocking_space.txt (used by Looking Glass, Ashen, Hearthian, Mansion, Tether)

### What Happens

Revisited spaces offer nothing new.

### Symptom

Players resent the map.

### Fix

Change something on each revisit: an enemy, a light, a sound, a new shortcut.
