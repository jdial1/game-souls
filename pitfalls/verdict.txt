## Pitfall: The Verdict

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Ampersand
**Found in:** souls/ampersand.txt (Ampersand)

### What Happens

The game interprets: personality types, compatibility readings, "what your answers say about you."

### Symptom

A reading becomes something one partner quotes at the other.

### Fix

Show both results and ask one question about them.
