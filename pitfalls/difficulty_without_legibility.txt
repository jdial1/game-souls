## Pitfall: Difficulty Without Legibility

**Area:** Feedback & Legibility
**Also known as:** Fake Difficulty (TV Tropes)
**Found in:** components/legible_failure.txt (used by Dwarven, Ashen, Hearthian, Spire, Mansion, Containment, Gauge, Casebook, Chorus, Swarm, Tapestry, Assembly, Tribunal)

### What Happens

Enemies are hard because of unfair hitboxes, off-screen attacks, or random damage spikes.

### Symptom

Players blame the game.

### Fix

Audit every playtest death; if the player can't name the cause, fix the tell.
