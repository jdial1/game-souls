## Pitfall: Theme as Paint

**Area:** Story & Discovery
**Found in:** components/theme_as_mechanic.txt (used by Loathing, Elysium, Mansion, Gauge, Ampersand, Casebook, Chorus, Tribunal)

### What Happens

The theme lives only in names, art, and flavor text; the mechanics underneath are generic.

### Symptom

The game is interesting for an hour and forgettable after.

### Fix

Apply the Integration Check.
