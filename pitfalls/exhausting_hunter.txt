## Pitfall: The Exhausting Hunter

**Area:** Systems & Rules
**Found in:** components/living_world.txt (used by Looking Glass, Dwarven, Ashen, Mansion, Tapestry, Tether)

### What Happens

An adaptive pursuer appears too often or too predictably.

### Symptom

Terror curdles into irritation.

### Fix

Give it rhythm, with long absences and escalation tied to player progress.
