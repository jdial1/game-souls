## Pitfall: The Wordy Tutorial

**Area:** Onboarding & Guidance
**Also known as:** Annoying Video Game Helper (TV Tropes)
**Found in:** components/trusting_the_player.txt (used by Looking Glass, Loathing, Ashen, Hearthian, Toybox, Containment, Gauge, Ampersand, Casebook, Assembly)

### What Happens

Text boxes explain what the level should teach.

### Symptom

Players skip the text and miss the mechanic.

### Fix

Redesign the first obstacle so the correct action is the natural one.
