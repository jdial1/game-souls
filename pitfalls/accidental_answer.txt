## Pitfall: The Accidental Answer

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Hearthian
**Also known as:** Luck-Based Mission (TV Tropes)
**Found in:** souls/hearthian.txt (Hearthian)

### What Happens

A player stumbles into a solution without understanding it.

### Symptom

Progress without an epiphany.

### Fix
