## Pitfall: Moon Logic

**Area:** Feedback & Legibility
**Also known as:** Guide Dang It! (TV Tropes)
**Found in:** components/legible_failure.txt (used by Dwarven, Ashen, Hearthian, Spire, Mansion, Containment, Gauge, Casebook, Chorus, Swarm, Tapestry, Assembly, Tribunal)

### What Happens

A puzzle solution makes sense only to the designer.

### Symptom

Players who look up the answer feel cheated rather than foolish.

### Fix

If the reaction is "How would anyone know that?", a clue is missing.
