## Pitfall: Additive Blandness

**Area:** Economy
**Found in:** components/synergy_engines.txt (used by Looking Glass, Loathing, Spire, Containment, Swarm)

### What Happens

Every piece is "+10% damage."

### Symptom

Choices feel interchangeable.

### Fix

Design pieces that change *how* you play, not just how much.
