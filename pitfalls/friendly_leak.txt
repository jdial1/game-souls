## Pitfall: The Friendly Leak

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Containment
**Found in:** souls/containment.txt (Containment)

### What Happens

A little heat disappears for free. It feels generous.

### Symptom

Designs that exploit the leak earn top stamina ratings, and players who reason from conservation get the wrong answer.

### Fix

Every sink is a part the player places; if the game wants to be generous, give them a better vent.
