## Pitfall: The Flavourful Lie

**Area:** Interface & Voice
**Found in:** components/interface_voice.txt (used by Containment, Gauge, Ampersand, Casebook, Chorus)

### What Happens

In-character copy claims something the game didn't do ("Officially certified").

### Symptom

Nobody notices until a player relies on it.

### Fix

Audit in-character claims against what the systems measure, and pin them with tests.
