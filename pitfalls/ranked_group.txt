## Pitfall: The Ranked Group

**Area:** Multiplayer & Social
**Found in:** components/social_safety.txt (used by Gauge, Ampersand)

### What Happens

Shared snapshots quietly become a scoreboard, a per-person tally appears, or a gift count becomes a ledger of who gave more.

### Symptom

Someone opens the game to see where they stand.

### Fix

Show changes as each person's own place growing, and any number as the group's.
