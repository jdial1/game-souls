## Pitfall: The Wall

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Hearthian
**Found in:** souls/hearthian.txt (Hearthian)

### What Happens

A player is stuck on one insight with no other thread to pull.

### Symptom

They quit or open a walkthrough, and the epiphany is lost forever.

### Fix

The knowledge graph must always leave an open question. After a long stretch without progress, the world may redirect their attention (a bird lands on the relevant statue, a distant light flickers) without ever giving the answer.
