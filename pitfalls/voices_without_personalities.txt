## Pitfall: Voices Without Personalities

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Elysium
**Found in:** souls/elysium.txt (Elysium)

### What Happens

Skills speak, but they all sound like the same narrator.

### Symptom

The build doesn't change the experience.

### Fix

Give each voice a distinct vocabulary, temperament, and agenda, and let them disagree with one another.
