## Pitfall: History Nobody Reads

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Dwarven
**Found in:** souls/dwarven.txt (Dwarven)

### What Happens

The game records rich legends and artifact histories, but they're buried in menus the player never opens.

### Symptom

Pillar 3 exists in the code but not in anyone's memory.

### Fix

Bring history to the player: engravings they walk past, named items that return in play, bards who sing about last year's disaster.
