## Pitfall: The Hoarder's Paralysis

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Tether
**Also known as:** Too Awesome to Use (TV Tropes)
**Found in:** souls/tether.txt (Tether)

### What Happens

Good gear feels too precious to use.

### Symptom

Stashes fill with gear nobody brings into raids.

### Fix

Cheap, viable kits, and wipes often enough that hoarding has no future.
