## Pitfall: The Pause Loophole

**Area:** Interface & Voice
**Found in:** components/diegetic_interface.txt (used by Looking Glass, Ashen, Mansion, Gauge, Tribunal, Tether)

### What Happens

One menu pauses while others don't, and players use it to cheat time.

### Symptom

The tension leaks.

### Fix

Be consistent about what stops the clock.
