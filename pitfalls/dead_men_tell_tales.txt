## Pitfall: Dead Men Tell Tales

**Area:** Multiplayer & Social
**Found in:** components/match_integrity.txt (used by Tribunal, Tether)

### What Happens

Eliminated players reach the living through spectating, kill-cams, or chat.

### Symptom

A dead player names the killer or the sniper's window, and the match collapses.

### Fix

Seal the dead from the living until the match ends.
