## Pitfall: Snowball Certainty

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Spire
**Found in:** souls/spire.txt (Spire)

### What Happens

Runs are effectively decided in the first ten minutes.

### Symptom

The rest of the run is a formality, won or lost.

### Fix

Escalate threats and change their type as the run progresses, so even strong starts must keep adapting.
