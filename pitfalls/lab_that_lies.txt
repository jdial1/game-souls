## Pitfall: The Lab That Lies

**Area:** Progression & Replay
**Found in:** components/workbench.txt (used by Containment, Assembly)

### What Happens

The lab's setup differs from the real game's.

### Symptom

A design that worked in the lab fails for real, and players stop trusting the tools.

### Fix

A test runs every example design in both and requires them to agree.
