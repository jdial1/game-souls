## Pitfall: Diegesis at the Cost of Legibility

**Area:** Interface & Voice
**Found in:** components/diegetic_interface.txt (used by Looking Glass, Ashen, Mansion, Gauge, Tribunal, Tether)

### What Happens

The player can't tell how hurt they are because the limp is too subtle.

### Symptom

Deaths feel unfair.

### Fix

Diegetic doesn't mean ambiguous; make states unmistakable (see components/legible_failure.txt).
