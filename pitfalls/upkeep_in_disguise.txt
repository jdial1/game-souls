## Pitfall: Upkeep in Disguise

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Gauge
**Found in:** souls/gauge.txt (Gauge)

### What Happens

A recurring charge on what the player already owns, defended as "only a stall."

### Symptom

A tester describes a stall as having lost something.

### Fix

Recurring sinks are things the player chooses to feed, never charges on what they hold.
