## Pitfall: Chores Forever

**Area:** Progression & Replay
**Found in:** components/automation.txt (used by Containment, Gauge, Swarm, Assembly)

### What Happens

Automation arrives long after the task stopped being interesting.

### Symptom

The first hours are remembered as a grind.

### Fix

Unlock the machine the moment the manual task stops teaching anything.
