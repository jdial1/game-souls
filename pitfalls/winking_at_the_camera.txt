## Pitfall: Winking at the Camera

**Area:** Story & Discovery
**Found in:** components/theme_as_mechanic.txt (used by Loathing, Elysium, Mansion, Gauge, Ampersand, Casebook, Chorus, Tribunal)

### What Happens

The game nudges the player to notice how clever, random, or deep it is.

### Symptom

The theme reads as self-conscious.

### Fix

The world must take itself completely seriously.
