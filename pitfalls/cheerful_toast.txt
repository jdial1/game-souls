## Pitfall: The Cheerful Toast

**Area:** Interface & Voice
**Also known as:** Annoying Video Game Helper (TV Tropes)
**Found in:** components/interface_voice.txt (used by Containment, Gauge, Ampersand, Casebook, Chorus)

### What Happens

Confirmations and celebrations pile on top of feedback the world already gave.

### Symptom

Players dismiss popups without reading, including the important ones.

### Fix

Delete every message the world already says.
