## Pitfall: Ludonarrative Whiplash

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Looking Glass
**Also known as:** Gameplay and Story Segregation (TV Tropes)
**Found in:** souls/looking_glass.txt (Looking Glass)

### What Happens

The player ghosts an entire level without a kill, and the following cutscene shows the hero gunning down guards.

### Symptom

The player's authorship is overwritten.

### Fix

Cutscenes must reflect how the player actually played, or stay out of the way.
