## Pitfall: Choice Without Consequence

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Elysium
**Found in:** souls/elysium.txt (Elysium)

### What Happens

Beliefs and failures are acknowledged once, then forgotten.

### Symptom

Players stop believing their choices matter.

### Fix

Track what the player has said and done and pay it off, even hours later, even in small ways.
