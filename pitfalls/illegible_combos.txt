## Pitfall: Illegible Combos

**Area:** Economy
**Found in:** components/synergy_engines.txt (used by Looking Glass, Loathing, Spire, Containment, Swarm)

### What Happens

Interactions are so opaque that nobody can reason about them.

### Symptom

Players pick at random.

### Fix

Keep each piece's own rule clear, even if the combinations are hidden.
