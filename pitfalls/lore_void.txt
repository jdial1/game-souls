## Pitfall: The Lore Void

**Area:** Story & Discovery
**Found in:** components/environmental_storytelling.txt (used by Looking Glass, Ashen, Hearthian, Mansion, Casebook)

### What Happens

Fragments are so scattered that no coherent story can be reconstructed.

### Symptom

The community stops theorizing.

### Fix

Write the full truth first.
