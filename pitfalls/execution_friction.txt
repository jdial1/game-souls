## Pitfall: Execution Friction

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Hearthian
**Found in:** souls/hearthian.txt (Hearthian)

### What Happens

Movement or controls are so clumsy that performing a known solution becomes the challenge.

### Symptom

Players understand what to do but can't do it.

### Fix

Once the player knows the answer, execution should be easy.
