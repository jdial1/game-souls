## Pitfall: Abandonment Disguised as Trust

**Area:** Onboarding & Guidance
**Found in:** components/trusting_the_player.txt (used by Looking Glass, Loathing, Ashen, Hearthian, Toybox, Containment, Gauge, Ampersand, Casebook, Assembly)

### What Happens

There's no guidance, and also no landmarks, no sightlines, and no clues.

### Symptom

Players wander aimlessly and quit.

### Fix

Removing arrows means adding world design, not just deleting UI.
