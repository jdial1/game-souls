## Pitfall: The Stagnant Mid-Game

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Swarm
**Found in:** souls/swarm.txt (Swarm)

### What Happens

The build matures at minute fifteen, the run lasts thirty, and enemies just become damage sponges.

### Symptom

The player stops paying attention.

### Fix

Introduce a new threat type exactly when the player gets comfortable.
