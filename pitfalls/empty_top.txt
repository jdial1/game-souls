## Pitfall: The Empty Top

**Area:** Progression & Replay
**Found in:** components/difficulty_ladder.txt (used by Loathing, Spire, Containment, Casebook, Chorus, Swarm)

### What Happens

Nothing is waiting for players who reach the top.

### Symptom

The most dedicated players leave.

### Fix

Efficiency leaderboards, restriction paths, and seeds give the top of the ladder somewhere to go.
