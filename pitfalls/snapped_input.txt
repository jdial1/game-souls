## Pitfall: The Snapped Input

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Chorus
**Found in:** souls/chorus.txt (Chorus)

### What Happens

The engine quantizes inputs onto the beat.

### Symptom

Sloppy play scores the same as precise play, and experts stop improving.

### Fix

Judge the window; never move the input.
