## Pitfall: The Audio-Log Dump

**Area:** Story & Discovery
**Found in:** components/environmental_storytelling.txt (used by Looking Glass, Ashen, Hearthian, Mansion, Casebook)

### What Happens

Environmental storytelling becomes a pile of voice recordings that explain the plot out loud.

### Symptom

The player stops looking at the environment.

### Fix

Let objects, placement, and architecture carry the story before text does.
