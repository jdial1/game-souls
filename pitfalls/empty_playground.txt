## Pitfall: The Empty Playground

**Area:** Onboarding & Guidance
**Found in:** components/trusting_the_player.txt (used by Looking Glass, Loathing, Ashen, Hearthian, Toybox, Containment, Gauge, Ampersand, Casebook, Assembly)

### What Happens

Curiosity stops paying off.

### Symptom

Players follow only the main path.

### Fix

Raise reward density or shrink the space. The designer should always get there first.
