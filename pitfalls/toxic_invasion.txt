## Pitfall: Toxic Invasion

**Area:** Multiplayer & Social
**Found in:** components/shared_discovery.txt (used by Loathing, Ashen, Hearthian, Containment, Tribunal)

### What Happens

Hostile multiplayer drives away the players it preys on.

### Symptom

Players go offline to avoid it.

### Fix

Make invasions opt-in or tied to a clear risk the player chose.
