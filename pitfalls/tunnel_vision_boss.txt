## Pitfall: The Tunnel-Vision Boss

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Looking Glass
**Found in:** souls/looking_glass.txt (Looking Glass)

### What Happens

A mandatory fight in a sealed arena that ignores the entire toolkit.

### Symptom

Players who built for stealth are suddenly stuck.

### Fix

Every major encounter must offer at least one non-lethal solution and one way to avoid it entirely.
