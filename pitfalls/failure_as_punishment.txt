## Pitfall: Failure as Punishment

**Area:** Failure & Stakes
**Found in:** components/failure_as_content.txt (used by Dwarven, Ashen, Hearthian, Elysium, Ampersand, Tapestry)

### What Happens

A failure simply closes a door with a flat "You fail."

### Symptom

Players save-scum every roll.

### Fix

Write every failure as a scene worth seeing.
