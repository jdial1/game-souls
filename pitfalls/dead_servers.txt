## Pitfall: Dead Servers

**Area:** Multiplayer & Social
**Found in:** components/shared_discovery.txt (used by Loathing, Ashen, Hearthian, Containment, Tribunal)

### What Happens

The communion depends on servers that eventually shut down.

### Symptom

The game becomes emptier than designed.

### Fix

Build an Offline Echo fallback from the start.
