## Pitfall: The Scoreboard

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Ampersand
**Found in:** souls/ampersand.txt (Ampersand)

### What Happens

Right guesses pay more, or a per-person tally appears.

### Symptom

Players guess safe, and the reveal turns tense instead of funny.

### Fix

Pay flat for doing it together, and keep any number about the pair ("84% of guesses landed").
