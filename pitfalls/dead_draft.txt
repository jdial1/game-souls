## Pitfall: The Dead Draft

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Swarm
**Found in:** souls/swarm.txt (Swarm)

### What Happens

Offers ignore what the player already owns, so most level-ups are irrelevant.

### Symptom

Drafting becomes clicking through.

### Fix

Weight offers toward the player's current weapons and recipes.
