## Pitfall: The Oracle

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Casebook
**Found in:** souls/casebook.txt (Casebook)

### What Happens

Feedback leaks: wrong letters flagged as typed, a check that refunds on success, an undo that returns a spent ration, or a control that behaves differently when a guess is right.

### Symptom

Players cycle letters, or read the controls instead of the page.

### Fix

Audit every surface that could reveal correctness; compare the screen with a right guess and a wrong one.
