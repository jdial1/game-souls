## Pitfall: The Camped Door

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Tether
**Found in:** souls/tether.txt (Tether)

### What Happens

Extracts are predictable, and players wait at them.

### Symptom

Raids are won by whoever camps the exit.

### Fix

Many doors, with variable availability and conditions.
