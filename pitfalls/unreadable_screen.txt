## Pitfall: The Unreadable Screen

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Swarm
**Found in:** souls/swarm.txt (Swarm)

### What Happens

Effects bury the player's character and the lethal projectiles.

### Symptom

Deaths feel random, even to experts.

### Fix

A visual hierarchy where threats always sit on top, and options to dim the player's own effects.
