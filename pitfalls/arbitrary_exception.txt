## Pitfall: The Arbitrary Exception

**Area:** Systems & Rules
**Found in:** components/systemic_consistency.txt (used by Looking Glass, Dwarven, Hearthian, Toybox, Containment, Gauge, Casebook, Chorus, Tapestry, Assembly)

### What Happens

A boss is immune, a door is indestructible for no reason, a scripted sequence ignores the rules.

### Symptom

Players stop experimenting because they no longer trust the world.

### Fix

No exceptions; if something must be blocked, block it with a rule the player can understand.
