## Pitfall: Checkpoint Sadism

**Area:** Space & Pacing
**Also known as:** Checkpoint Starvation (TV Tropes)
**Found in:** components/interlocking_space.txt (used by Looking Glass, Ashen, Hearthian, Mansion, Tether)

### What Happens

The run back from a checkpoint to the challenge is long, tedious, and dangerous for no reason.

### Symptom

Players quit from boredom, not difficulty.

### Fix

Keep run-backs short, or make them a learnable challenge with their own shortcut.
