## Pitfall: The Buzzer

**Area:** Interface & Voice
**Found in:** components/audio_information.txt (used by Mansion, Containment, Chorus, Tether)

### What Happens

Mistakes trigger loud, unpleasant sounds.

### Symptom

Players mute the game, and with it the game's best information.

### Fix

A mistake removes a sound; it never adds a punishing one.
