## Pitfall: The Solved Simulation

**Area:** Systems & Rules
**Found in:** components/living_world.txt (used by Looking Glass, Dwarven, Ashen, Mansion, Tapestry, Tether)

### What Happens

Players find a strategy that makes them immune to the world's pressure.

### Symptom

The world stops producing stories.

### Fix

Escalate external pressure over time and let internal systems (aging, grudges, decay) erode even perfect defenses.
