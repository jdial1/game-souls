## Pitfall: The Red Signal

**Area:** Interface & Voice
**Found in:** components/interface_voice.txt (used by Containment, Gauge, Ampersand, Casebook, Chorus)

### What Happens

Warning colours or words contradict the mechanics.

### Symptom

Players read a full store or a quiet line as a problem.

### Fix

Reserve warning styling for real danger.
