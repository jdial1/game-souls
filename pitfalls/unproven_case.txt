## Pitfall: The Unproven Case

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Casebook
**Found in:** souls/casebook.txt (Casebook)

### What Happens

The player proves every line but never decides anything about the crime.

### Fix

At the end of each arc, let the player file a finding built from lines they proved, checked by the same rationed rules.
