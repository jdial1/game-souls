## Pitfall: The Rerun

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Tapestry
**Found in:** souls/tapestry.txt (Tapestry)

### What Happens

The same events fire again and again.

### Symptom

Players click through without reading.

### Fix

A deep event library, and events that remember they've happened.
