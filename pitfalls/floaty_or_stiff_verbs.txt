## Pitfall: Floaty or Stiff Verbs

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Toybox
**Found in:** souls/toybox.txt (Toybox)

### What Happens

The core action is technically functional but not joyful.

### Symptom

Playtesters stop moving around between objectives.

### Fix

Go back to the gray box; no new content until the empty room is fun.
