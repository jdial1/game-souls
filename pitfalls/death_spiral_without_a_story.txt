## Pitfall: The Death Spiral Without a Story

**Area:** Failure & Stakes
**Found in:** components/failure_as_content.txt (used by Dwarven, Ashen, Hearthian, Elysium, Ampersand, Tapestry)

### What Happens

One bad event cascades into inevitable collapse with nothing interesting along the way.

### Symptom

Players quit the moment things go wrong.

### Fix

Give the fall dramatic beats and desperate choices.
