## Pitfall: The Checkerboard Forever

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Containment
**Found in:** souls/containment.txt (Containment)

### What Happens

One layout is best at every tier.

### Symptom

Shared codes converge, and nobody redesigns after an upgrade.

### Fix

Upgrades that move ratios, and tiers that change the shape of the heat, not only its size.
