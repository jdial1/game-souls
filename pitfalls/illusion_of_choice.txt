## Pitfall: The Illusion of Choice

**Area:** Space & Pacing
**Found in:** components/interlocking_space.txt (used by Looking Glass, Ashen, Hearthian, Mansion, Tether)

### What Happens

Several routes that all converge on the same scripted door.

### Symptom

Players stop exploring alternatives.

### Fix

Let routes change the state of the world or what the player learns.
