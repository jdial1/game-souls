## Pitfall: The Spaghetti Wall

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Assembly
**Found in:** souls/assembly.txt (Assembly)

### What Happens

The factory becomes too tangled to change, and rebuilding is punished.

### Symptom

Players abandon saves rather than refactor.

### Fix

Free deconstruction and blueprints, so rebuilding is cheaper than living with the mess.
