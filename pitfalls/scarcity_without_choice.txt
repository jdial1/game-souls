## Pitfall: Scarcity Without Choice

**Area:** Economy
**Found in:** components/scarcity_economy.txt (used by Dwarven, Loathing, Spire, Mansion, Containment, Ampersand, Casebook, Swarm, Tapestry, Assembly, Tether)

### What Happens

Resources are tight, but there's only one way to spend them.

### Symptom

Scarcity is just a tax.

### Fix

Every resource needs at least two competing uses.
