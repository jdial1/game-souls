## Pitfall: Monetization Creep

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Loathing
**Also known as:** Bribing Your Way to Victory (TV Tropes)
**Found in:** souls/loathing.txt (Loathing)

### What Happens

Revenue pressure pushes purchases from tradable convenience toward pay-to-win or FOMO.

### Symptom

Veteran trust erodes quickly and permanently.

### Fix

Hold the Mr. Accessory standard absolutely, even when it costs money.
