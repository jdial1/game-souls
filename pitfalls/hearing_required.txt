## Pitfall: Hearing Required

**Area:** Interface & Voice
**Found in:** components/audio_information.txt (used by Mansion, Containment, Chorus, Tether)

### What Happens

A cue exists only as sound.

### Symptom

Deaf players can't play at all.

### Fix

Give every critical cue a visual twin.
