## Pitfall: The Game Plays Itself

**Area:** Progression & Replay
**Found in:** components/automation.txt (used by Containment, Gauge, Swarm, Assembly)

### What Happens

Automation reaches the decisions, or the player's build is so complete that nothing is left to decide.

### Symptom

Players put the controller down and watch.

### Fix

Automate verbs, never choices, and keep introducing a new problem the automation can't solve.
