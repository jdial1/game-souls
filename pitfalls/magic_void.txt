## Pitfall: The Magic Void

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Assembly
**Found in:** souls/assembly.txt (Assembly)

### What Happens

Items teleport into a global inventory.

### Symptom

The logistics puzzle evaporates, and the game becomes a clicker.

### Fix

Everything travels through physical space.
