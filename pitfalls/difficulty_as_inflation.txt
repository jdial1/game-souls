## Pitfall: Difficulty as Inflation

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Ashen
**Found in:** souls/ashen.txt (Ashen)

### What Happens

The team makes the game "harder" by inflating enemy health and damage instead of deepening patterns.

### Symptom

Fights become long attrition slogs rather than tense exams.

### Fix

Difficulty comes from reading and punishing patterns, not from bigger numbers.
