## Pitfall: Arbitrary Syncopation

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Chorus
**Found in:** souls/chorus.txt (Chorus)

### What Happens

An obstacle sits on an off-beat with no musical cue.

### Symptom

The hit feels random and unfair.

### Fix

If you want an off-beat hit, add a hi-hat or a synth stab to the track to justify it.
