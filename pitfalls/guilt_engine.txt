## Pitfall: The Guilt Engine

**Area:** Multiplayer & Social
**Found in:** components/social_safety.txt (used by Gauge, Ampersand)

### What Happens

Streaks with teeth, escalating reminders, and notifications that name a debt ("Your cousin needs wood!", "Maya is waiting").

### Symptom

Players feel they owe the group their attention, and the game becomes something they nag each other about.

### Fix

Apply the Bad-Month Test; say what is waiting, never who is to blame or who needs what.
