## Pitfall: The Toxic Lobby

**Area:** Multiplayer & Social
**Found in:** components/match_integrity.txt (used by Tribunal, Tether)

### What Happens

In-game betrayal spills into real abuse.

### Symptom

Players leave after one bad match.

### Fix

Moderation tools, and a game frame that ends the betrayal at the reveal.
