## Pitfall: Illegible Chaos

**Area:** Feedback & Legibility
**Found in:** components/legible_failure.txt (used by Dwarven, Ashen, Hearthian, Spire, Mansion, Containment, Gauge, Casebook, Chorus, Swarm, Tapestry, Assembly, Tribunal)

### What Happens

The simulation produces rich events, but the player can't see the causes.

### Symptom

Players describe their losses as random.

### Fix

Surface causes, not just effects.
