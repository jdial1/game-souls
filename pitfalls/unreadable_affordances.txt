## Pitfall: Unreadable Affordances

**Area:** Systems & Rules
**Found in:** components/systemic_consistency.txt (used by Looking Glass, Dwarven, Hearthian, Toybox, Containment, Gauge, Casebook, Chorus, Tapestry, Assembly)

### What Happens

The player can't tell what can be stacked, burned, or climbed.

### Symptom

Players only discover creative solutions by watching videos.

### Fix

A consistent visual language, taught early.
