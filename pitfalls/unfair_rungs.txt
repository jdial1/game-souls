## Pitfall: Unfair Rungs

**Area:** Progression & Replay
**Found in:** components/difficulty_ladder.txt (used by Loathing, Spire, Containment, Casebook, Chorus, Swarm)

### What Happens

Higher tiers are harder only through bigger numbers and randomness.

### Symptom

Experts plateau and quit.

### Fix

Each rung should demand a new skill or a new decision, not just more patience.
