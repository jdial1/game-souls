## Pitfall: Obscurity as a Shortcut

**Area:** Story & Discovery
**Also known as:** Guide Dang It! (TV Tropes)
**Found in:** components/environmental_storytelling.txt (used by Looking Glass, Ashen, Hearthian, Mansion, Casebook)

### What Happens

Designers confuse "cryptic" with "deep" and hide critical progression behind unguessable actions.

### Symptom

Players need a wiki to finish the main path.

### Fix

The main path must be discoverable by observation alone; keep the truly obscure secrets optional.
