## Pitfall: Visual Desync

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Chorus
**Found in:** souls/chorus.txt (Chorus)

### What Happens

An animation takes 30 frames to play, but the beat lands at frame 25.

### Symptom

Players fail despite knowing the rhythm.

### Fix

Animations snap to the beat, even if it means dropping frames to catch up.
