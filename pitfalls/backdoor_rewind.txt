## Pitfall: The Backdoor Rewind

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Containment
**Found in:** souls/containment.txt (Containment)

### What Happens

A save import or cloud restore undoes a meltdown.

### Symptom

The receipt stops mattering.

### Fix

Refuse it in Hardcore, and mark the run as restored everywhere else.
