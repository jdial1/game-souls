## Pitfall: The Hoarding Trap

**Area:** Economy
**Also known as:** Too Awesome to Use (TV Tropes)
**Found in:** components/scarcity_economy.txt (used by Dwarven, Loathing, Spire, Mansion, Containment, Ampersand, Casebook, Swarm, Tapestry, Assembly, Tether)

### What Happens

Players are so afraid of running out that they never use anything and finish the game with a full inventory.

### Symptom

Scarcity creates boredom, not tension.

### Fix

Create pressure to spend (spoilage, carrying limits, encounters that punish holding back).
