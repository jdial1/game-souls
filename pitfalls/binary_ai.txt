## Pitfall: Binary AI

**Area:** Systems & Rules
**Found in:** components/living_world.txt (used by Looking Glass, Dwarven, Ashen, Mansion, Tapestry, Tether)

### What Happens

Enemies flip straight from idle to hostile.

### Symptom

Stealth becomes pass/fail with no tension.

### Fix

Awareness ladders with readable intermediate states.
