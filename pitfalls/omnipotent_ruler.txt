## Pitfall: The Omnipotent Ruler

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Tapestry
**Found in:** souls/tapestry.txt (Tapestry)

### What Happens

The player has too much direct control, and subordinates never disobey.

### Symptom

The game becomes easy once the economy snowballs.

### Fix

Institutional inertia; the larger the realm, the harder it is to steer.
