## Pitfall: The Safe Pocket

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Tether
**Found in:** souls/tether.txt (Tether)

### What Happens

The unlootable container is too big.

### Symptom

Players grab the best loot, stash it, and die on purpose to leave quickly.

### Fix

Keep it tiny, and tie value to extraction.
