## Pitfall: The Soft Lock

**Area:** Economy
**Also known as:** Unwinnable by Design (TV Tropes)
**Found in:** components/scarcity_economy.txt (used by Dwarven, Loathing, Spire, Mansion, Containment, Ampersand, Casebook, Swarm, Tapestry, Assembly, Tether)

### What Happens

The player saves with no resources in front of a mandatory challenge.

### Symptom

They must restart hours of progress.

### Fix

Every mandatory encounter has an evasion route or a guaranteed supply nearby.
