## Pitfall: The Idle Dead

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Tribunal
**Found in:** souls/tribunal.txt (Tribunal)

### What Happens

Eliminated players have nothing to do.

### Symptom

They leave, and matches empty out early.

### Fix

Give the dead something to do that can't leak information: tasks, a ghost vote, a spectator role.
