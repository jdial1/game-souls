## Pitfall: Monster Overexposure

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Mansion
**Found in:** souls/mansion.txt (Mansion)

### What Happens

The creature is shown in full light early and often.

### Symptom

By the midpoint, the monster is a known quantity and fear has drained away.

### Fix

Ration visibility; let the player's imagination do the work for as long as possible.
