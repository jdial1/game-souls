## Pitfall: The Decorative Mix

**Area:** Interface & Voice
**Found in:** components/audio_information.txt (used by Mansion, Containment, Chorus, Tether)

### What Happens

Music and effects are layered for mood, but none of them tell the player anything.

### Symptom

Players play with sound off and lose nothing.

### Fix

Audit every threat and state change; each needs an audible cue.
