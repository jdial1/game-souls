## Pitfall: The Cheater Epidemic

**Area:** Multiplayer & Social
**Found in:** components/match_integrity.txt (used by Tribunal, Tether)

### What Happens

Stakes grow but security doesn't.

### Symptom

Every loss is blamed on cheaters, and many of them are.

### Fix

Server authority and anti-cheat sized to the stakes.
