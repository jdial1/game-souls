## Pitfall: The Loudest Wins

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Tribunal
**Found in:** souls/tribunal.txt (Tribunal)

### What Happens

Discussion is dominated by whoever talks most.

### Symptom

Quiet players are voted out for being quiet.

### Fix

Structure the talking phase (speaking order, timers, private claims).
