## Pitfall: The Sinkhole Economy

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Assembly
**Found in:** souls/assembly.txt (Assembly)

### What Happens

Production outpaces consumption, and chests fill with useless items.

### Symptom

The factory stops moving.

### Fix

An infinite sink, so the factory always has a reason to consume.
