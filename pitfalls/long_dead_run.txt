## Pitfall: The Long Dead Run

**Area:** Failure & Stakes
**Also known as:** Unwinnable (the "walking dead" state) (TV Tropes)
**Found in:** components/failure_as_content.txt (used by Dwarven, Ashen, Hearthian, Elysium, Ampersand, Tapestry)

### What Happens

A run or save is doomed by the midpoint but takes another thirty minutes to actually end.

### Symptom

Players abandon instead of finishing.

### Fix

Let lost games end fast, or add comeback mechanics that keep the ending tense.
