## Pitfall: The Screensaver

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Gauge
**Found in:** souls/gauge.txt (Gauge)

### What Happens

Nothing is lost and nothing is wanted.

### Symptom

Players open the game, see that everything is fine, and close it.

### Fix

Every visit should find something that wants something. Wanting, not owing.
