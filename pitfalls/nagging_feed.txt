## Pitfall: The Nagging Feed

**Area:** Multiplayer & Social
**Found in:** components/social_safety.txt (used by Gauge, Ampersand)

### What Happens

News repeats until it's acknowledged.

### Symptom

Good news starts to feel like a notification badge.

### Fix

News is spent the first time it is shown, and never re-announced.
