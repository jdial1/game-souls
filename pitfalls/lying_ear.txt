## Pitfall: The Lying Ear

**Area:** Interface & Voice
**Found in:** components/audio_information.txt (used by Mansion, Containment, Chorus, Tether)

### What Happens

Occlusion or panning is wrong, so enemies sound like they're somewhere they aren't.

### Symptom

Players die "to a sound from the wrong floor" and stop trusting audio.

### Fix

Test positional audio as rigorously as hitboxes.
