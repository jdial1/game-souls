## Pitfall: The Chat Room

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Ampersand
**Found in:** souls/ampersand.txt (Ampersand)

### What Happens

The reveal grows a reply box, then a thread, then a notification per message.

### Symptom

The conversation happens typed instead of in the other room, and the "we talked about it" mark gets rarer.

### Fix

One line each, then the instruction to go and talk.
