## Pitfall: Walls of Undifferentiated Text

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Elysium
**Found in:** souls/elysium.txt (Elysium)

### What Happens

Long passages with no choices, no checks, and no stakes.

### Symptom

Players skim.

### Fix

Keep conversations interactive; every few lines should offer a meaningful choice, a check, or an inner voice reacting.
