## Pitfall: Depth Without an On-Ramp

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Loathing
**Found in:** souls/loathing.txt (Loathing)

### What Happens

The math is so dense that new players can't find the fun.

### Symptom

Only veterans stay, and the community stops growing.

### Fix

Respect player literacy, but keep early resource constraints generous and let the brutal optimization pressure arrive with ascension.
