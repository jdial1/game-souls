## Pitfall: Jump-Scare Addiction

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Mansion
**Found in:** souls/mansion.txt (Mansion)

### What Happens

Loud stings replace atmosphere.

### Symptom

Players are startled but not afraid, and the scares become predictable.

### Fix

Spend silence generously and save shocks for moments that break an established pattern.
