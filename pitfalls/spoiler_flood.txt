## Pitfall: The Spoiler Flood

**Area:** Multiplayer & Social
**Found in:** components/shared_discovery.txt (used by Loathing, Ashen, Hearthian, Containment, Tribunal)

### What Happens

Discoveries leak within hours and everyone else reads the answer instead of finding it.

### Symptom

The mystery dies on day one.

### Fix

Layer secrets so the deepest ones require in-game action, not just information.
