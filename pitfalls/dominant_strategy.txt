## Pitfall: The Dominant Strategy

**Area:** Economy
**Also known as:** Game-Breaker (TV Tropes)
**Found in:** components/synergy_engines.txt (used by Looking Glass, Loathing, Spire, Containment, Swarm)

### What Happens

One build or tool wins so reliably that everyone converges on it.

### Symptom

Every playthrough looks the same.

### Fix

Give tools costs or counters, and make the pieces of the top build rarely appear together.
