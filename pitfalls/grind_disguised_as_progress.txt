## Pitfall: Grind Disguised as Progress

**Area:** Progression & Replay
**Also known as:** Level Grinding (TV Tropes)
**Found in:** components/difficulty_ladder.txt (used by Loathing, Spire, Containment, Casebook, Chorus, Swarm)

### What Happens

Meta-progression becomes a stat treadmill, so players win by playing longer rather than better.

### Symptom

Skill matters less than hours logged.

### Fix

Unlock options, not raw power, or make the power optional.
