## Pitfall: The Waiting Room

**Area:** Systems & Rules
**Found in:** components/living_world.txt (used by Looking Glass, Dwarven, Ashen, Mansion, Tapestry, Tether)

### What Happens

NPCs stand in place until the player arrives, then begin performing.

### Symptom

The world feels like a stage set.

### Fix

Give every NPC at least one routine that runs without the player.
