## Pitfall: Mechanics That Contradict the Theme

**Area:** Story & Discovery
**Found in:** components/theme_as_mechanic.txt (used by Loathing, Elysium, Mansion, Gauge, Ampersand, Casebook, Chorus, Tribunal)

### What Happens

The story says the protagonist is desperate; the systems give them infinite resources.

### Symptom

The player stops believing the fiction.

### Fix

Check each system against the tone (axes/tone.txt, "Tone Killers").
