## Pitfall: The Stopwatch Takeover

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Casebook
**Found in:** souls/casebook.txt (Casebook)

### What Happens

Speed becomes the prestige: the clock shows tenths, the board ranks by time, and reading the frame costs seconds.

### Symptom

Players skip the story and guess.

### Fix

Rank by help first and time second, and stop the clock while the player reads the case.
