## Pitfall: The Blob

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Tapestry
**Found in:** souls/tapestry.txt (Tapestry)

### What Happens

One realm grows until nothing can threaten it.

### Symptom

The last hundred years are a formality.

### Fix

Growth creates resistance: coalitions, internal instability, succession crises that split large realms.
