## Pitfall: The Short Memory

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Ampersand
**Found in:** souls/ampersand.txt (Ampersand)

### What Happens

Everything is archived, but nothing old enough comes back.

### Symptom

"on this day" only ever shows last month, and the history screen is visited once.

### Fix

Budget storage and loading for years, and bring the past forward unasked.
