## Pitfall: The Overstayed Gimmick

**Area:** Soul-Specific: Toybox
**Found in:** souls/toybox.txt (Toybox)

### What Happens

A mechanic appears unchanged space after space.

### Symptom

Players sigh when it shows up again.

### Fix

Introduce, develop, twist, conclude, retire.
