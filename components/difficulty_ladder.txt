## Component: Difficulty Ladder

**The principle:** Finishing the game is only the beginning. Winning unlocks a harder, stranger way to play, and mastery is measured by how high the player climbs.

**Used by:** Loathing, Spire, Containment, Casebook, Chorus, Swarm

**Requires:** Structure: Runs or Rebirth Account (a ladder needs repeated attempts)
**Supports:** synergy_engines, shared_discovery
**Conflicts With:** Structure: Single Journey (there's nothing to climb after the credits); social_safety (leaderboards and ranking)

### Design Rules

* **Each Rung Is Earned:** Winning unlocks a harder tier with a stacked modifier (more elite enemies, less healing, a starting curse). The player can always see which rung they've reached.

* **The Rebirth Loop:** Completing a run lets the player restart at the beginning, carrying one permanent perk or skill into a different class or build (Loathing's "permed" skills).

* **Restriction Runs:** Provide built-in paths that strip away comfort.
  * *Examples (Loathing):* Hardcore (no access to saved storage), Teetotaler (no booze), Oxygenarian (no food or drink at all), or a thematic overhaul where every enemy is replaced with bees.

* **Efficiency Is Prestige:** Measure status not by whether the player won, but by how few turns, days, or minutes it took.

* **Seeded Runs and Daily Challenges:** Let players share seeds and compete on the same daily run. Identical starting conditions turn every result into a comparable test of skill.

* **Opt-In Difficulty:** If meta-progression makes the game easier over time, pair it with a system that lets experts add difficulty back (*Hades*' Heat).

### Tuning by Soul

* **Spire:** The ladder is a stack of difficulty modifiers applied to a self-contained run.
* **Loathing:** The ladder is a rebirth economy. Each ascension adds permanent breadth, and restriction paths add self-imposed friction.
* **Containment:** The ladder is a rebirth economy plus restriction runs, and the top is stamina: the most output from a design that holds.
* **Casebook:** The ladder lives inside each installment. A harder second page opens after the first, harder because one named rule changes.
* **Chorus:** The ladder is speed, syncopation, and strictness of timing; mastery is a perfectly chained combo.
* **Swarm:** Curse levels, heat modifiers, and endless and speedrun modes stack on top of a clock that always wins eventually.

### What It Costs

* **Tuning Every Rung:** Twenty difficulty tiers is more tuning work than one game.
* **The Content Treadmill:** Players who climb the ladder burn through content. Long-term health depends on new pieces, modes, and paths.
* **Balance Debt:** Every permanent perk interacts with everything ever released. Power creep is cumulative and hard to reverse.

### How It Fails

* **Grind Disguised as Progress** (pitfalls/grind_disguised_as_progress.txt): Meta-progression becomes a stat treadmill, so players win by playing longer rather than better.
* **Unfair Rungs** (pitfalls/unfair_rungs.txt): Higher tiers are harder only through bigger numbers and randomness.
* **The Empty Top** (pitfalls/empty_top.txt): Nothing is waiting for players who reach the top.
